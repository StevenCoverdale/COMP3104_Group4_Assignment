George Brown College is a public college of applied arts and technology located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. 
It was established in 1967 and offers a wide range of programs including certificates, diplomas, degrees, and postgraduate studies. 

The college is known for its strong industry connections, hands-on learning, and focus on career readiness. 
Major areas of study include business, health sciences, engineering technologies, hospitality, and the arts. 
The main campuses are St. James, Casa Loma, and Waterfront.

Website: https://www.georgebrown.ca/

George Brown College collaborates with leading employers to provide students with valuable work-integrated learning opportunities. 
The college supports student success through services such as academic advising, career counseling, and accessible learning resources. 
With a diverse student population, George Brown fosters an inclusive and vibrant campus community. 
Alumni of the college contribute to various industries locally and globally.
