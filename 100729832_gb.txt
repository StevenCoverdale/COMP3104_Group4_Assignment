George Brown College is a public college of applied arts and technology located in 
Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It was established in 1967 and offers a wide range of programs 
including certificates, diplomas, degrees, and postgraduate studies. The college is known 
for its strong industry connections, hands-on learning, and focus on career readiness. 
Major areas of study include business, health sciences, engineering technologies, hospitality,
 and the arts. The main campuses are St. James, Casa Loma, and Waterfront.
