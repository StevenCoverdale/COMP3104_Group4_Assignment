Devops Key Principles and Practices:
- Collaboration: Fosters a culture where development and operations teams work together, sharing ownership and responsibility for the entire software lifecycle. 
- Automation: Automates routine tasks in the build, test, and deployment processes, leading to faster and more frequent releases. 
- Continuous Integration/Continuous Delivery (CI/CD): Automates code integration, testing, and delivery to a production environment, enabling continuous value delivery. 
- Feedback Loops: Emphasizes rapid feedback from users and systems to inform and improve future development cycles, promoting a continuous improvement mindset. 
- Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Treats infrastructure (servers, networks, etc.) as code, allowing for consistent, versioned, and automated management of environments.
