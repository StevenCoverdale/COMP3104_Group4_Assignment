Toronto, Ontario - Canada's Largest City
==========================================

Toronto is the capital city of the province of Ontario and the largest city in Canada by population. 
It is a vibrant, multicultural metropolis located on the northwestern shore of Lake Ontario.

Key Facts
---------

- Population: Over 2.9 million (city proper), over 6.4 million (Greater Toronto Area)
- Founded: 1793 (as York), Incorporated as Toronto in 1834
- Province: Ontario
- Country: Canada
- Nickname: "The 6ix", "T.O.", "Hogtown"

Geography & Climate
-------------------

Toronto is situated on a broad sloping plateau intersected by an extensive network of rivers, deep ravines, and urban forest. 
The city experiences a humid continental climate with warm, humid summers and cold winters.

- Location: Southeastern Ontario, on the shore of Lake Ontario
- Elevation: 76.5 meters (251 feet) above sea level
- Area: 630.2 km² (243.3 sq mi)

Economy
-------

Toronto is Canada's financial and business capital, hosting the headquarters of the country's five largest banks and many corporate headquarters.

Major Economic Sectors:
- Finance and banking
- Technology and innovation
- Film and television production
- Tourism and hospitality
- Healthcare and education

Culture & Attractions
---------------------

Toronto is one of the most culturally diverse cities in the world, with over 200 ethnic groups and more than 180 languages spoken.

Popular Attractions:
- CN Tower - iconic landmark and once the world's tallest free-standing structure
- Royal Ontario Museum (ROM)
- Toronto Islands
- Ripley's Aquarium of Canada
- Distillery District
- Casa Loma
- St. Lawrence Market
- Toronto Zoo
- Hockey Hall of Fame

Education
---------

Toronto is home to several renowned educational institutions:
- University of Toronto - one of the world's top research universities
- York University
- Ryerson University (now Toronto Metropolitan University)
- George Brown College
- Seneca College
- Centennial College

Transportation
--------------

Toronto has an extensive public transportation system operated by the Toronto Transit Commission (TTC):
- Subway system with four lines
- Streetcar network
- Bus routes throughout the city
- GO Transit for regional connections

Toronto Pearson International Airport is Canada's busiest airport and a major international hub.

Sports & Entertainment
----------------------

Toronto is home to several major professional sports teams:
- Toronto Maple Leafs (NHL - Hockey)
- Toronto Raptors (NBA - Basketball) - 2019 NBA Champions
- Toronto Blue Jays (MLB - Baseball) - 1992, 1993 World Series Champions
- Toronto FC (MLS - Soccer)
- Toronto Argonauts (CFL - Football)

Government
----------

Toronto operates under a single-tier municipal government structure:
- Mayor-council system
- City Council with 25 councillors
- Part of the Greater Toronto Area (GTA)

Notable Facts
-------------

- Toronto is consistently ranked as one of the world's most livable cities
- It has the highest concentration of artists in Canada
- The city hosts the Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF), one of the world's premier film festivals
- Toronto's PATH is the world's largest underground shopping complex
- The city is a major center for music, theatre, motion picture production, and television production
